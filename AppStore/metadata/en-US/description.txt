Move files, folders, images, links and text between apps without rearranging your windows.

Drag something to the side of a display and Borda keeps it in a temporary glass shelf. When you need it, move the pointer back to that spot and drag the item into any other app.

• Multiple independent shelves across displays
• Several items in the same shelf
• Files, folders, images, links, plain text and rich text
• Quick Look, Share, ZIP, image resize and OCR
• Optional clipboard history and watched folders
• Launch at login and global shortcuts
• Local storage with no account or cloud

Borda is free. Optional coffees support its continued development but never unlock features.
